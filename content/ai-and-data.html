Your data and AI, in plain terms

Your data stays yours. It sits in systems set up for your firm alone, and it is never used to train an AI model. This page shows where it lives, who can see it, where AI is involved and how you leave.

Who can see your data

  • Your people, at the role your admins set, and Jackson Bates of DataBates, to build and support your tool.
  • The providers named on this page, plus Google Drive and email (Apple iCloud Mail, Gmail) for the files and messages you send us.
  • No one else. Your data, figures and names are never used for another client, in a demo or in marketing.

Where AI is involved, and where it is not

  • Building. We use Claude, from Anthropic, to write code, load data and draft documents, so it reads the files you send us. Model training is off, and no other AI service touches your data without your written OK.
  • Inside a tracker or dashboard. No AI. Risk flags and due dates come from fixed rules, not from a model.
  • Agents and briefs. Optional. They run in your firm's own AI account. AI output is always a draft for your team to review.

Your part, and how you leave

  • What to send. Only what the job needs: no Social Security numbers, bank details or tax returns unless the scope calls for them. Confirm any client consent your professional rules or tax law (Section 7216) require.
  • Export any time. A full export of your data is yours on request, member or not.
  • Never locked out. A partner at your firm holds a sealed admin login from the first day.
  • A clean handoff. When a membership ends, we move the hosting, database and code to accounts you control, with the admin login, the Owner's Guide and a one-hour walkthrough.
  • Deleted on request, and told fast. We delete our working files at the end of a project when you ask. If someone reaches your data in our care without permission, we tell you within three business days.
